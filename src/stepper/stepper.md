# Photolithography Stepper

The stepper is the patterning tool. It projects a demagnified image of a mask
onto a photoresist-coated wafer, exposes one field, then *steps* the stage to the
next field and repeats until the whole wafer is patterned. This is how circuit
geometry is transferred onto the wafer.
