# Photoresist Spin Coater

The spin coater lays down thin, uniform films of liquid chemistry on a wafer by
dispensing a puddle at the center and spinning the wafer at high speed so
centrifugal force spreads it into an even coat. It is used mainly for photoresist,
and also for spin-on dopant films that feed the [furnace](../furnace/furnace.md)
drive-in step.
