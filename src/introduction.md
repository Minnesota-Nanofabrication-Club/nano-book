# Understanding DIY wafer equipment design from scratch, for beginners.

Hello!, and welcome to this book. This book will teach you how to make semiconductor manufacturing equipment on your own, in your own garage. There is no prerequisites needed in order to read, understand and build projects from this book. Having prior knowledge in general engineering or wafer manufacturing will be useful.

Your safety is one of the most important factors to keep in mind when reading this book. Lab equipment can be dangerous. Some processes use may highly dangerous chemicals and gases. Never do anything unless you understand your own safety, and have been trained on safe operation of these hazards.
