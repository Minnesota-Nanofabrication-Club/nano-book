# Furnace

The furnace is a high-temperature tube furnace used for two processes: **spin-on
dopant (SOD) drive-in**, where a high-temperature bake diffuses dopant atoms from
a spun-on film into the silicon to form junctions, and **thermal oxidation**,
where heating silicon in an oxygen or steam ambient grows a controlled layer of
silicon dioxide.
